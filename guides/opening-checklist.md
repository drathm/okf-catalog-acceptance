1. Switch on both deck ovens and set them to 240 °C; they take forty minutes to reach temperature.
2. Take the overnight dough out of the walk-in and shape the first batch.
3. Check the par-baked loaves from the day before against their labels (see [Par-bake](../terms/par-bake.md)) and bin anything past its date.
4. Start the coffee machine and run one flush cycle.
5. Count the float, unlock the door at 07:00, and turn the sign.
