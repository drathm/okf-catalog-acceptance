The proofing window at Tidewater is 50 minutes at 26 °C, measured from the moment the loaf is shaped. A loaf that has waited more than 65 minutes is binned, not baked: it collapses in the oven and the crumb goes gummy. The window was settled at the October production meeting after the morning shift's trial, and it applies to every loaf except the rye, which proofs for 35 minutes.
